# GENRE PROFILE: VLOG / SHORT-FORM

## Overview
Fast-paced, dynamic 9:16 portrait video for mobile platforms (TikTok, Reels, Shorts). Rhythm, engaging visuals, and crisp typography take priority over rigid product fidelity.

## Structure
1. **Strong Visual Hook (3-5s)**: Immediate movement, question, or bold visual to stop scrolling.
2. **Fast-Paced Beats (3-5s each)**: Quick transitions, varied angles (first-person POV, reaction, b-roll).
3. **Punchy Outro (3s)**: Call to follow, like, or summary thought.

## Key Rules
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 Portrait default.
- **Rhythm & Fast Cuts**: Clips are short (3-5s each). Keep viewer engagement high.
- **Heavy Subtitle Emphasis**: Prominent animated or centered Korean subtitles on every sentence.
- **Background Music**: Dynamic, upbeat beat throughout the entire video.
